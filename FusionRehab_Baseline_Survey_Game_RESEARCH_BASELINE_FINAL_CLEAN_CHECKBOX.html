Protocol used here
Order is fixed: RIGHT side first — Abduction, Adduction, Internal Rotation, External Rotation, Flexion, Extension — then LEFT side in the same six-movement order. Only the required side's index fingertip is accepted. After the fingertip enters the solid pink centre of DESTINATION, the current movement is saved automatically and the next movement gets a new movement-specific START → DESTINATION route. Route deviations reset the movement.
